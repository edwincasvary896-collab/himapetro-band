ABOUT THE SONG

I Need Your Confession

A song about a son who keeps trying to earn his mother's pride while feeling constantly compared, misunderstood, and never good enough.

Behind the anger and frustration is something deeper: the fear of losing the person he loves, and the desperate need to hear that his efforts were not meaningless.

THE STORY BEHIND THE SONG

Based on a True Story

I Need Your Confession is inspired by a true story about a son's struggle to earn his mother's pride and recognition while facing constant comparison and the fear of losing her.
