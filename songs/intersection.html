ABOUT THE SONG

Intersection

A song about a son trapped between two choices: demanding an end to the constant comparison, or staying because he cannot bear to lose his mother.

After years of pressure, disappointment, and feeling unacknowledged, he reaches a point where he questions what a mother expects from her child and whether he will ever be enough.

THE STORY BEHIND THE SONG

Based on a True Story

Intersection is inspired by a true story about a son's struggle between wanting to walk away from a painful relationship and still wanting the love and recognition of his mother.
