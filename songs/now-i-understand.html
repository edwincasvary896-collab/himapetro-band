ABOUT THE SONG

Now I Understand

A song about finally accepting that not everyone can become what someone else wants them to be.

After years of comparison, pressure, and trying to earn his mother's approval, the son begins to understand that his worth cannot depend entirely on someone else's expectations.

Sometimes, walking away is not about giving up. Sometimes, it is the only way to stop hurting and finally see yourself clearly.

THE STORY BEHIND THE SONG

Based on a True Story

Now I Understand is inspired by a true story about the realization that constantly seeking someone's approval can become a painful cycle, and that sometimes letting go is necessary to find peace and understand your own worth.
